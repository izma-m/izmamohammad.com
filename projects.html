Biomedical simulation

SIM-IE turns biomedical equipment into something you can explore

Status: concept and planning blueprint, October 2026

SIM-IE is a proposed biomedical engineering simulation and learning platform. It aims to help learners connect theory to how biomedical equipment and systems behave, through guided, interactive and repeatable experiences, especially where physical equipment, specialist labs or mentors are hard to reach.

It is designed to complement, not replace, physical laboratories, supervised clinical exposure and manufacturer-authorised training. The plan is to begin with one tightly scoped, measurable learning experience, test it with learners and educators, then grow from the evidence toward virtual labs, scenario-based practice, educator tools and, later, institutional and manufacturer-supported simulations.

Founded and led by Izma Mohammad, and presented at IBFT'26 (SSUET & UTM).

EdTech research

XAI-STEM: teaching the algorithm to explain itself

Status: research design, August 2026

How can Explainable AI help STEM teachers make personalised, data-informed decisions while seeing clear reasons behind every recommendation? XAI-STEM combines a literature review with three planned experiments: benchmarking predictive models on a public STEM education dataset, benchmarking explanation methods such as SHAP and LIME, and evaluating the explanations with teachers.

Izma serves as Product Progression Director.

Adaptive learning

PAEIS: the intelligence behind ExamSlayer

Status: proposed specification v1.0, October 2026

The Personalized Adaptive Exam Intelligence System keeps an evolving picture of a learner's knowledge, misconceptions, exam technique and retention, then recommends the next most useful action: a question, feedback or a revision task.

It is the framework beneath ExamSlayer, aimed first at CAIE IGCSE and International A-Level students. It is a modular system, not a single language model, and its benefits remain to be tested.

Game-based revision

Prep Hop: exam revision at running speed

Status: draft requirements v1.0, October 2026

A browser-based endless runner with a pastel bunny. When an obstacle approaches, the game freezes and asks an exam-style question; a correct answer makes the bunny hop. It follows five IGCSE and O Level courses, randomises numbers each round, and ends each run with a report card by topic.

Game-based learning

Aina Blues escapes the maths temple

Status: product document v1.0, October 2026

An escape-room game for Grade 7 to 9 mathematics. Players guide Aina Blues, a white Turkish Angora cat detective, through a temple, choosing answers by running through one of four doors, earning XP, unlocking outfits and getting a printable report card. It is a single web page, easy to embed in school sites.
