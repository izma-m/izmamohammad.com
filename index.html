Karachi, Pakistan.

Karachi is a melting pot of everything: dust, traffic, noise, chaos, and parks, trees, baarish and food.

On any stressful day, "ek cup doodh patti" melts the stress away. Driving here is a chore, in fact, an unmatched experience. Every ride is a mystery as we weave through tiny, hidden residential lanes that only God knows the end of, and somehow, in an hour or so, we reach home.

What keeps Karachi going is the belief in collective survival, and biryani. When the heavy rains come and the streets flood, nobody waits for the authorities. Locals are out with ropes, drenched to the bone, pushing strangers' stalled cars for nothing in return.

That resilient, stubborn sense of collective survival is why I love this city. It is tough and raw, with a massive heart. Even as a Karachiite, I can't say I know all of Karachi. It is far too big.
